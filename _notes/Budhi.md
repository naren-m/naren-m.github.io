# Budhi (बुद्धि)

**Budhi** (also spelled Buddhi) refers to intellect, wisdom, and the power of discernment in Sanskrit philosophy.

> [[Budhi]]r Balam Yasho dhairya, nirbhayathvam arogatham ajaadyam vaakpatuthvam cha, Hanumanth Smaranath Bhavaeth

*By remembering Hanuman, one attains intelligence (Budhi), strength (Balam), fame (Yasho), courage (Dhairya), fearlessness (Nirbhayathvam), good health (Arogatham), alertness (Ajaadyam), and eloquence (Vaakpatuthvam).*

## In Yoga and Vedanta

Budhi is one of the four aspects of the **antahkarana** (inner instrument):
- **[[Manas]]** - mind (processing thoughts)
- **Budhi** - intellect (discrimination and decision-making)
- **[[chitta]]** - memory/consciousness
- **Ahamkara** - ego/sense of self
