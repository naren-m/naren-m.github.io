# Tulsidas

**Goswami Tulsidas** (1532-1623) was a Hindu saint, poet, and philosopher renowned for his devotion to Lord Rama. He is best known as the author of the [[Shri Ram Charit Manas]] and the [[Hanuman Chalisa]].

He composed his works in [[Awadhi]], making the stories of the Ramayana accessible to the common people of northern India.

## Major Works

- [[Shri Ram Charit Manas]] - Epic retelling of the Ramayana in Awadhi
- [[Hanuman Chalisa]] - 40-verse devotional hymn to Hanuman
