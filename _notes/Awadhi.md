# Awadhi

**Awadhi** is a sublanguage (boli) of Hindi, primarily spoken in the Awadh region of Uttar Pradesh, India - mainly in Faizabad, Sultanpur, Ambedkar Nagar, Pratapgarh, Amethi, Gonda, and parts of Basti.

The word "Awadhi" is derived from **Awadh**, and the purest form of the language can be found in Ayodhya, Faizabad, Ambedkar Nagar, and Sultanpur.

## Literary Significance

The [[Shri Ram Charit Manas]] (including the [[Hanuman Chalisa]]) was composed by [[Tulsidas]] in Awadhi, making it one of the most important literary languages in the devotional tradition.
