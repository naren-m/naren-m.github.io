# Shri Ram Charit Manas

**Shri Ram Charit Manas** (श्रीरामचरितमानस) is an epic poem in [[Awadhi]], composed by [[Tulsidas]] in the 16th century. It retells the story of the Ramayana and is one of the most important works in Hindi literature.

The [[Hanuman Chalisa]], one of the most widely recited Hindu prayers, is part of this larger work.
